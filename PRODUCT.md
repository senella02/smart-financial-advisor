# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Novice investors with limited time researching Thai and US common stocks.

## Product Purpose

Help users understand market developments and research stocks through evidence and explanations so they can make their own decisions. The product does not execute trades or give definitive buy/sell instructions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The [functional requirements](docs/functional-requirements.md) are authoritative for product scope, acceptance criteria, languages, publication and refresh rules, chat history, and portfolio accounting. [Open questions](docs/requirements-questions.md) remain unresolved until an owner decision is recorded. Follow [source precedence](docs/source-notes.md#source-precedence) when material conflicts.

## Product Principles

- Never invent financial figures, citations, provider capabilities, or accounting rules.
- Distinguish missing data and source timestamps from application refresh times.
- Keep private portfolio/profile data out of shared AI outputs and caches.

Keep this file limited to confirmed, durable product rules and links to their authoritative requirements. Implementation status belongs in task plans such as `plan.md`. Temporary experiments, scaffold choices, and observed code are not product rules. Establish design conventions separately through requested design work.
